SECTION 07Interview Q&A — Node.js jobs

Q1What is the average salary of a Node.js developer in India?

The average salary is ₹4-7 LPA for freshers, ₹6-10 LPA for junior developers, ₹10-15 LPA for mid-level, and ₹15-25 LPA for senior developers. Full-stack Node.js developers earn significantly more.

Q2What skills do I need for a Node.js job?

Core Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB or SQL, REST APIs, and JavaScript. React skills are highly valued. DevOps and cloud knowledge can boost your salary significantly.

Q3Is Node.js a good career choice in India?

Yes — Node.js is one of the best backend technologies for a career in India. It's in high demand, pays well, and offers excellent career growth opportunities.

Q4Which companies hire Node.js developers in India?

Major companies like Flipkart, Paytm, Ola, Swiggy, and thousands of startups hire Node.js developers. Product-based companies pay the highest salaries.

Q5What is the future of Node.js in India?

The future is very bright. Node.js adoption is growing rapidly in Indian tech companies. With digital transformation and microservices adoption, demand will continue to increase.

SECTION 09Frequently asked questions

How many Node.js jobs are there in India?

There are over 25,000+ Node.js job postings in India in 2026, with 10,000+ new jobs added each year. The number is growing rapidly.

Is Node.js better than Python for backend?

Both are excellent. Node.js is better for real-time, I/O-heavy applications. Python is better for data-intensive applications. Node.js has a stronger job market in startups and product companies.

How long does it take to get a Node.js job?

With 3-6 months of dedicated learning and portfolio building, you can land a Node.js job. A structured training program with placement support can accelerate this.

Can I work remotely as a Node.js developer?

Yes — Node.js is one of the most remote-friendly technologies. Many Indian and global companies hire remote Node.js developers.
