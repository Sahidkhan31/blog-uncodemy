Software Testing Salary Guide: What Freshers Earn in 2026

AK Ankit Kaushik · Lead Trainer, Data Engineering & AI | Updated 14 September 2026 | 14 min read Free · Career Guide

Quick summary — software testing fresher salary 2026

Software testing freshers in India earn between ₹3 LPA and ₹7 LPA in 2026, depending on manual vs automation skills. Manual testers typically start at ₹3-5 LPA, while automation testers with Selenium and Java/Python skills start at ₹4-7 LPA. Freshers with strong portfolios and automation skills can push to ₹8-10 LPA, and top product companies pay ₹10-14 LPA for exceptional candidates.

  4. Company-wise pay — product companies vs. service companies.
  5. Salary by experience — how pay grows over time.
  6. Skills that boost salary — what gets you paid more.
  7. How to negotiate — tips to maximize your first offer.

SECTION 01Manual vs automation salary gap

The biggest factor in software testing salary is whether you do manual or automation testing. The gap is significant and grows over time.

Role Fresher Salary 3-5 Years
Manual Tester ₹3 – ₹5 LPA ₹5 – ₹8 LPA
Automation Tester (Selenium) ₹4 – ₹7 LPA ₹8 – ₹14 LPA
SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) ₹6 – ₹10 LPA ₹12 – ₹22 LPA
Performance Tester (JMeter) ₹4 – ₹7 LPA ₹8 – ₹15 LPA

Why the gap exists:

  • Automation scales: One automation script can run thousands of tests. Manual testing doesn't scale.
  • Coding skills: Automation requires programming (Java, Python, JavaScript), which commands higher pay.
  • SDET roles pay more: SDETs combine testing and development skills, making them more valuable to employers.
  • Manual is commoditized: Manual testing is easier to learn, so there's more supply and lower pay.
Key insight: If you're entering software testing, learn automation. The salary difference is 30-50% at the fresher level and grows to 100%+ over time.

SECTION 02Average fresher salary in 2026

Here's what freshers actually earn in software testing in 2026, broken down by role type.

Manual testing freshers:

  • Typical range: ₹3-5 LPA, with ₹3.5-4.5 LPA being the most common.
  • Top paying: ₹6-7 LPA at product companies.
  • Service companies: ₹3-4 LPA.

Automation testing freshers:

  • Typical range: ₹4-7 LPA, with ₹5-6 LPA being common for candidates with Selenium and Java/Python skills.
  • Top paying: ₹8-10 LPA at product companies.
  • SDET roles: ₹6-10 LPA for freshers with strong coding skills.

What this means for you:

  • A fresher with only manual testing skills starts at ₹3-4 LPA.
  • A fresher with Selenium and Java/Python skills starts at ₹4-7 LPA.
  • A fresher with SDET-level skills (coding + testing) starts at ₹6-10 LPA.
Pro tip: Even if you start in manual testing, learn automation within the first year. The salary jump when you switch to automation is 30-50%.

SECTION 03City-wise salary comparison

Location changes your salary. Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR pay significantly more than smaller cities.

City Manual Fresher Automation Fresher
Bangalore ₹4 – ₹6 LPA ₹5 – ₹8 LPA
Hyderabad ₹3.5 – ₹5 LPA ₹4.5 – ₹7 LPA
Delhi NCR (Noida/Gurgaon) ₹3.5 – ₹5 LPA ₹4.5 – ₹7 LPA
Mumbai ₹3.5 – ₹5 LPA ₹4.5 – ₹6.5 LPA
Pune ₹3 – ₹4.5 LPA ₹4 – ₹6 LPA
Chennai ₹3 – ₹4.5 LPA ₹4 – ₹6 LPA
Tier-2/3 Cities ₹2.5 – ₹4 LPA ₹3.5 – ₹5 LPA

Key takeaways:

  • Bangalore pays the highest for both manual and automation testers.
  • Hyderabad and Delhi NCR offer competitive pay with slightly lower cost of living.
  • Remote roles often pay based on the company's location, not yours — a Bangalore company may pay Bangalore rates even if you're in a Tier-2 city.
Pro tip: If you can relocate or work remotely for a Bangalore-based company, you can earn 20-30% more than local market rates.

SECTION 04Company-wise pay

The company you join matters as much as your skills. Product companies, fintech, and analytics-driven firms pay significantly more than traditional IT services.

Company types and typical fresher pay:

  • Product companies and startups: ₹5-10 LPA for automation testers, with ESOPs and annual hikes of 15-25%.
  • Fintech and BFSI: ₹6-12 LPA for SDET roles.
  • Analytics-driven firms: ₹5-9 LPA.
  • Traditional IT services: ₹3-5 LPA, with slower growth.
  • Consulting firms: ₹4-7 LPA.

Top-paying companies for software testers in India:

  • Microsoft, Amazon, Google (₹12-20 LPA for experienced roles)
  • Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato (₹8-15 LPA)
  • Deloitte, EY, PwC (₹6-12 LPA)
  • Accenture, Cognizant, Infosys (₹4-8 LPA)
Key insight: The company you choose for your first job sets your salary trajectory. A product company role at ₹6 LPA can outpace a service company role at ₹4 LPA within two years.

SECTION 05Salary by experience level

Software testing salaries grow steadily with experience. Here is the typical progression based on 2026 data.

Experience Manual Range Automation Range SDET Range
0-1 year (Fresher) ₹3 – ₹5 LPA ₹4 – ₹7 LPA ₹6 – ₹10 LPA
1-3 years ₹4 – ₹7 LPA ₹6 – ₹10 LPA ₹8 – ₹14 LPA
3-6 years ₹6 – ₹10 LPA ₹10 – ₹18 LPA ₹12 – ₹22 LPA
6-9 years ₹8 – ₹14 LPA ₹15 – ₹25 LPA ₹18 – ₹30 LPA
9+ years ₹12 – ₹20 LPA ₹20 – ₹35 LPA ₹25 – ₹45 LPA

What drives salary growth:

  • Automation skills: Moving from manual to automation is the biggest salary jump.
  • Coding skills: SDET roles that require strong programming pay significantly more.
  • Company switches: Switching companies every 2-3 years typically yields 25-40% hikes.
  • Specialization: Performance testing, security testing, and cloud testing command premiums.
  • Certifications: ISTQB, AWS, and cloud certifications add 10-20% to salaries.
Pro tip: The biggest salary jump happens when you move from manual to automation, or from a service company to a product company. Plan for that move after 1-2 years.

SECTION 06Skills and negotiation

Skills determine your salary more than years of experience. Here's what to learn and how to negotiate.

High-impact skills for freshers:

  • Selenium: The most in-demand automation tool. Learn with Java or Python.
  • Java or Python: Programming skills are essential for automation roles.
  • API testing: Postman and REST Assured are increasingly expected.
  • CI/CD: Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or GitLab CI.
  • Cloud testing: AWS, Azure, or GCP familiarity.
  • Performance testing: JMeter or LoadRunner.
  • ISTQB certification: Adds credibility and can boost your salary.

Negotiation tips:

  • Know your worth: Research salary ranges for your city, skills, and company type.
  • Highlight your portfolio: If you have a GitHub with automation projects, mention it. It justifies a higher ask.
  • Ask for the range first: When HR asks your expectation, ask "What is the budgeted range for this role?" first.
  • Be specific: "Based on my Selenium and Java skills and the Bangalore market rate, I was expecting ₹5-6 LPA."
  • Negotiate beyond salary: If base pay is fixed, negotiate signing bonus, relocation, or early performance review.
Pro tip: A fresher with automation skills and a portfolio can negotiate ₹1-2 LPA more than the initial offer. Practice your pitch before the call.

SECTION 08Frequently asked questions

What is the average starting salary for a software tester in India?

Manual testers typically start at ₹3-5 LPA, while automation testers with Selenium and Java/Python skills start at ₹4-7 LPA. SDET roles pay ₹6-10 LPA for freshers with strong coding skills.

Is automation testing better paid than manual testing?

Yes. Automation testers earn 30-50% more than manual testers at the fresher level, and the gap grows to 100%+ over time. Learn Selenium and a programming language to maximize your salary.

Which city pays software testers the most in India?

Bangalore pays the highest, followed by Hyderabad and Delhi NCR. Remote roles for Bangalore-based companies often pay Bangalore rates even if you live elsewhere.

What skills boost software testing salary the most?

Selenium, Java/Python, API testing (Postman, REST Assured), CI/CD, and cloud testing are the highest-impact skills. ISTQB certification adds credibility and can boost your salary.

How much can a fresher negotiate in software testing?

A fresher with automation skills and a strong portfolio can negotiate 10-20% above the initial offer — roughly ₹1-2 LPA more. Research your market rate and practice your pitch.
