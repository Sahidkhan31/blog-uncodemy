Best Deep Learning Course in Noida: Full Comparison Guide 2026

AK Ankit Kaushik · Lead Trainer, Full Stack & Web Development | Updated 12 September 2026 | 19 min read Free · Comparison Guide

Quick answer — which course is best?

For most career-focused learners, a job-oriented bootcamp (like Uncodemy) offers the best balance of practical skills, portfolio projects, and placement support at a reasonable price. University degrees (Amity, NIET) are better if you want a formal qualification. Short courses (ProgNXT, NobleProg) are best if you already have a job and want to add a specific skill.

In this comparison guide you will learn:

  1. Bootcamps vs. university vs. short courses — the three main options in Noida.
  2. Top institutes compared — fees, duration, and what each offers.
  3. Placement support reality check — what "placement assistance" actually means.
  4. How to choose — based on your background, budget, and goal.

SECTION 01Three Course Types (3 compared)

T1Bootcamps — fastest path to job-ready skills

What they are: Intensive, project-based training programs run by private institutes. Typically 3–9 months, focused on practical skills and portfolio building.

Examples in Noida: Uncodemy, DataMites, Gyansetu, Softcrayons.

Typical fees: ₹15,000–50,000 depending on duration and depth. DataMites' 9-month AI course is at the higher end[citation:4], while Gyansetu's 6-month AI course is more affordable[citation:9].

Best for: Career switchers, freshers, and working professionals who want job-ready skills fast without a degree.

Trade-off: Less theoretical depth than a university degree, but far more practical than a short course.

T2University Programs — deepest theoretical foundation

What they are: Formal degree or diploma programs from universities. Typically 2–4 years for full degrees, with deep coverage of theory, mathematics, and research.

Examples in Noida: Amity University (M.Tech Data Science with Advanced Deep Learning)[citation:1], NIET (B.Tech AIML)[citation:8], Maharishi University (B.Tech AI & ML)[citation:3], JSS University (CSE AI & ML)[citation:7].

Typical fees: ₹1.5L–5L+ per year depending on the program. NIET's B.Tech AIML totals around ₹5.29 lakh for 3 years[citation:8].

Best for: Students who want a formal qualification, research careers, or roles at large enterprises that require degrees.

Trade-off: Expensive and time-consuming. Not practical if you need to start earning quickly.

T3Short Courses — skill-specific, quick, affordable

What they are: Brief, focused training programs on specific deep learning topics or tools. Typically 14–21 hours over a few days or weeks.

Examples in Noida: ProgNXT (Deep Learning with Keras, DeepLearning4J, Deep Neural Networks)[citation:5][citation:10][citation:15], NobleProg (Stable Diffusion training)[citation:2].

Typical fees: ₹5,000–25,000 depending on the provider. NobleProg's Stable Diffusion course is significantly more expensive (₹2.5L+), targeting enterprise clients[citation:2].

Best for: Working professionals who already have a job and want to add a specific deep learning skill to their toolkit.

Trade-off: Not enough for a career switch on their own. You'll need to combine multiple short courses or pair them with a bootcamp.

SECTION 03Placement Support Reality (2 points)

P1What "placement assistance" actually means

The honest truth: "Placement assistance" varies wildly between institutes. Some provide genuine interview opportunities; others just help with resume formatting and call it placement support.

What to look for: Ask specific questions — How many hiring partners? What's the average salary of placed students? Can you speak to recent alumni? Do they guarantee interviews or just "assistance"?

Red flag: If an institute promises "100% placement guarantee," be skeptical. No institute can guarantee a job — they can only provide opportunities and support.

Good sign: Gyansetu claims 500+ hiring partners and offers mock interviews, resume building, and Naukri profile help[citation:9]. Uncodemy provides interview preparation and hands-on projects[citation:6].

P2Portfolio and projects matter more than placement claims

The honest truth: Even the best placement support won't help if you don't have projects to show. Recruiters hire based on demonstrated skills, not certificates.

What to prioritize: Choose a course that includes real projects, capstone work, and portfolio building. DataMites offers 10 capstone projects and 1 client project[citation:4]; Uncodemy emphasizes hands-on coding and live projects[citation:6].

What to avoid: Courses that are mostly theory or certificate-focused without substantial project work. You'll struggle in interviews.

SECTION 04How to Choose (Decision Guide)

Here's a simple decision framework based on your situation:

  • If you're a fresher or career switcher: Choose a bootcamp with strong placement support and project work. Uncodemy, DataMites, or Gyansetu are good options.
  • If you want a formal degree: Consider Amity, NIET, or Maharishi University. Expect higher fees and longer duration, but deeper theoretical foundation.
  • If you already have a job and want a specific skill: Take a short course from ProgNXT or NobleProg. Quick, affordable, and focused.
  • If your budget is tight: Short courses or affordable bootcamps (Gyansetu) are better than expensive university programs.
  • If you want the fastest path to a job: Bootcamps typically get you job-ready in 3–9 months, vs. years for a degree.
Pro tip: Before enrolling, ask for the contact details of 2–3 recent alumni. Ask them: Did the course actually help you get interviews? Were the projects substantial? Would you recommend it? This one step will save you from a bad investment.

SECTION 06Frequently asked questions

What is the best deep learning course in Noida for beginners?

For beginners, a job-oriented bootcamp like Uncodemy offers the best balance of practical training, projects, and placement support. If you want a formal degree, consider Amity or NIET — but expect higher fees and longer duration.

How much do deep learning courses cost in Noida?

Fees range from ₹15,000–50,000 for bootcamps, ₹5,000–25,000 for short courses, and ₹1.5L–5L+ per year for university programs. Your budget and career goals should guide the choice.

Do I need a degree to get a deep learning job?

No — many deep learning roles are filled by candidates with bootcamp training and strong portfolios. What matters most is demonstrated project experience, not the credential.

How long does it take to learn deep learning?

With a structured bootcamp, you can build job-ready deep learning skills in 6–9 months. University programs take longer but provide deeper theoretical grounding.
